Folio I — The Gathering

Where inquiry has always looked like art.

There are festivals, and then there is Techfest. Since 1998, the students of IIT Bombay have staged a yearly reunion between two disciplines the modern world insists on separating — the precision of the machine and the wonder of the old masters. Under one campus sky, robotics arenas share the ground with speaker halls, and a night of demonstrations sits beside a morning of quiet, difficult work.

Est. 1998 · Powai, Mumbai
